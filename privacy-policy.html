Privacy policy

Our commitment to data privacy

Protecting the security and privacy of your personal data is important to us; therefore, we conduct our business in compliance with applicable laws on data privacy protection and data security. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Personal data

Through our website, Hi5 Solutions will not collect any personal data about you (e.g. your name, address, telephone number or email address) unless you voluntarily choose to provide it, give your consent, or unless otherwise permitted by applicable laws and regulations for the protection of your personal data.

How we use data

When you provide personal data, we usually use it to respond to your inquiry, process your request or give you access to specific information or offers. To support our customer relationship with you:

  • We may store and process personal data and share it with trusted third parties to better understand your business needs and improve our products and services.
  • We (or a third party on our behalf) may use personal data to contact you about a Hi5 Solutions offer or to conduct online surveys to better understand our customers’ needs.

If you choose not to have your personal data used to support our customer relationship (especially direct marketing or market research), we will respect your choice. We do not sell or otherwise market your personal data to third parties, except to Hi5 Solutions affiliates.

Purpose limitation

Hi5 Solutions will collect, use or disclose personal data supplied by you online only for the purposes disclosed to you, unless the disclosure:

  • is for an additional purpose directly related to the original purpose for which the data was collected,
  • is necessary to prepare, negotiate and perform a contract with you,
  • is required by law or by competent governmental or judicial authorities,
  • is necessary to establish or preserve a legal claim or defence,
  • is necessary to prevent fraud or other illegal activities, such as wilful attacks on Hi5 Solutions information technology systems.

Communications and usage data

When you use telecommunications services to access our website, your communications data (e.g. IP address) or usage data (e.g. the beginning, end and extent of each access) are technically generated and could conceivably relate to personal data. Where there is a compelling necessity, this data will be collected, processed and used in accordance with the applicable data privacy legal framework.

Non-personal data collected automatically

When you access our website, we may automatically collect non-personal data (e.g. browser type and operating system, the referring website, number of visits, average time spent and pages viewed). We may use this data and share it with trusted third parties to monitor and improve our website’s performance and content.

Cookies

We may store some data on your computer in the form of a cookie to recognise your device on your next visit. Cookies help us tailor the website to your interests. If you do not wish to receive cookies, configure your browser to erase or block them, or to warn you before a cookie is stored.

Links to other websites

Our website contains links to other websites. Hi5 Solutions is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of other websites.

Questions

For questions about this policy, email us at info@hi5solutions.in. We may update this policy at any time; please review it regularly.
