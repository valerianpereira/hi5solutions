Air treatment with ozone reduces airborne bacteria and removes unpleasant odours in malls, hotels, clubs, resorts, hospitals, offices, public utilities, food processing, pharma and cold storage.

It helps tackle sick building syndrome, reduces toxic VOCs, and is effective against food pests and for deep cleaning.

Water treatment with ozone reduces organic impurities — useful for swimming pools, kitchens, sewage, waste water and laundry — and cuts the use of chlorine and bromine.
