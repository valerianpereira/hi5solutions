Play flooring

Because children fall — and should land softly.

  • EPDM
  • SBR
  • Rubber tiles
  • Artificial grass

Playground flooring is slip-resistant and cushions falls, helping prevent serious injury while children run and play. Our range covers EPDM rubber granules, rubber tiles and artificial grass.

We choose the flooring after checking your requirement and the existing surface. The base needs to be concrete or asphalt, with a border at the boundaries.

Suitable forPlaygrounds, gardens, indoor play areas, schools, clubs, hotels, resorts, corporate and builder residential projects, and housing societies.
